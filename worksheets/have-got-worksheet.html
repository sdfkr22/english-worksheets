have got / has gotGrammar

Aynı şeyi iki şekilde söylemek: be ↔ have got

be ile
My eyes are blue.
have got ile
I have got blue eyes. (= I've got blue eyes.)

Üç şey değişir:

1. Özne değişir: My eyes → I  ·  Her hair → She  ·  His nose → He
2. Sıfat ismin önüne gelir: are blue → blue eyes (✗ eyes blue)
3. Tekil sayılabilir isimde a / an gerekir: His nose is big. → He's got a big nose.
Çoğul isimlerde (eyes, ears, shoes) ve sayılamayanlarda (hair) a kullanılmaz.

Negatives (Olumsuz) — haven't got / hasn't got

I / you / we / they  haven't got  (= have not got)

he / she / it  hasn't got  (= has not got)

Olumsuzda any kullanılır: I haven't got any brothers or sisters.
✗ I don't have got …  ·  ✓ I haven't got … / I don't have …
