My School Day

by Zeynep, 4-B

Hello! My name is Zeynep and I am ten years old. I am a student at Atatürk Primary School in Eskişehir.

My school is big and old. There are twenty-four classrooms, a library and a small garden. My classroom is on the first floor. It is bright and clean. There are twelve desks and a big board in it. My class is 4-B. We are twenty-two students.

My favourite subject is science because it is fun. Maths is difficult for me, but my teacher is very patient. Her name is Mrs Aydın. She is young and friendly.

School starts at eight o'clock. We have a long break at eleven. At break time my best friend Melis and I are always in the garden. We are happy at school!
