The Verb “to be”: am / is / are & there is / there are

Singular or plural? · expressions with be · negatives · questions · describing places

Name:Class:Date:

Watch out! Singular or plural? (Dikkat: tekil mi, çoğul mu?)

+ is  news, maths, money, hair, information, advice, water, bread, everybody, nobody, everything

+ are  trousers, jeans, glasses, scissors, clothes, people, children, men, women

These words look plural but take is: The news is on at seven. · These words look singular but take are: The people are friendly.
Türkçede tek kelime olanlar İngilizcede çoğul olabilir (trousers = pantolon → are), çoğul görünenler tekil olabilir (news → is).

Expressions with be (be ile kullanılan kalıplar)

be hungry / thirsty / tired / cold  ·  be late (for)  ·  be ready

be good at  ·  be afraid of  ·  be interested in  ·  be full of  ·  be right / wrong

In English we say I am 12 years old — never “I have 12 years”. (Yaş söylerken be kullanılır.)

Negatives (Olumsuz cümleler)

Long formShort form(s)Example
I am notI'm notI'm not interested in cars.
he / she / it is notisn't · he's notThe shop isn't open on Sundays.
you / we / they are notaren't · we're notWe aren't ready yet.
there is / are notthere isn't · there aren'tThere aren't any eggs.

There is no “amn't”. One negative is enough: There isn't any bread, not “There isn't no bread”.

Questions with be (be ile soru)

be + subject?   Are your parents at work? · Is there a bank near here?

question word + be + subject?   Why aren't you at school? · How many students are there in your class? · What is your new school like?

Question wordYou ask about …Example answer
Wherea placeIn the library.
How oldageShe's thirteen.
How muchpriceTen liras.
What … likea descriptionHe's friendly and funny.
Whya reasonBecause she's ill.

Short answers: Yes, I am. / No, I'm not. We never shorten the yes answer (“Yes, I'm.” ✗).
