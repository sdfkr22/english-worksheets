The Verb “to be”: am / is / are

Name & age · family · school · feelings — Level: A1 (10 years old)

Name:Class:Date:

Remember! (Hatırla!)

Subjectto beShort formExample
IamI'mI am ten years old.
he / she / itishe's / she's / it'sShe is my sister.
you / we / theyareyou're / we're / they'reThey are in the garden.

One person or thing → is  ·  two or more → are  ·  only “I” takes am.
Tek kişi/şey → is, birden fazla → are, sadece “I” ile → am.

Negative: add not (Olumsuz: not ekle)

Long formShort formExample
I am notI'm notI'm not tired.
he / she / it is notisn'tShe isn't at school.
you / we / they are notaren'tThey aren't my friends.

Careful! There is no “amn't”. We always say I'm not.
Dikkat: “amn't” yoktur, her zaman I'm not deriz.

I These sentences are wrong! Write a negative sentence and then a true one.

Bu cümleler yanlış. Önce olumsuz, sonra doğru cümleyi yaz. Örnek: Snow is hot. → Snow isn't hot. It is cold.
  1. Elephants are small.
  2. The sun is blue.
  3. Fish are in the sky.
  4. A banana is square.

Questions: change the order (Soru: sırayı değiştir)

He is your brother. → Is he your brother?

You are ready. → Are you ready?

QuestionYes …No …
Am I late?Yes, you are.No, you aren't.
Is she your sister?Yes, she is.No, she isn't.
Are they at home?Yes, they are.No, they aren't.

In short answers we never say “Yes, I'm.” — we say Yes, I am.
Kısa cevaplarda olumlu cevabı kısaltmayız.
